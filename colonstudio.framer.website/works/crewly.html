Crewly is an AI-powered customer service platform that lets growing companies deploy a crew of customer support agents (not just one chatbot) — each trained for different tasks like billing, onboarding, technical support, and order issues — helping teams deliver fast, human-like support at scale.

Crewly was built for companies and ecommerce brands that are growing too fast for human-only support and want to reduce ticket volume without compromising customer experience.
